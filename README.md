# GTA VI Trophy Guide

A complete, single-page trophy guide for Grand Theft Auto VI — every trophy in one place, with a practical step-by-step guide for each one, so you can plan your playthrough and chase the Platinum without missing anything.

## What is inside

- **55 trophies, fully covered:** 1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver and 46 Bronze. Every trophy comes with its own guide — what to do, where to go, and what to watch out for.
- **Progress tracker:** tick off the trophies you have already earned. Your progress is saved in your browser (local storage), so it is still there when you come back — no account or sign-in needed.
- **Roadmap:** a suggested order for tackling the list, so the long grind trophies can be started early and the missable ones are never left to chance.
- **Spoiler protection mode:** hide story details until you are ready to see them. Turn it on to browse the list safely before or during your first playthrough.
- **Search and filters:** find any trophy in seconds, and narrow the list by tier (Platinum / Gold / Silver / Bronze), by type, or to just the missable ones.

## Run it

### Option 1 — serve it locally

```bash
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in your browser.

### Option 2 — build it to an output directory

```bash
node main.ts
```

This copies the app into the output directory, ready to be served as a static site.
