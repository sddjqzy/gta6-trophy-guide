⚠ The one missable trophy

Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at all 18 spots. Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1 disappears after that chapter, and GTA VI is reported to have no mission replay. Do that spot in Chapter 1, before the chapter ends. Everything else can be finished in free roam after the story. Source: OutputLag GTA 6 trophy list; KeenGamer.

Save habit: keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission — there is reportedly no New Game Plus either (OutputLag).

Roadmap to the Platinum — 5 stages, about 200 hours

  1. Stage 1 — Story. Play Chapters 1–4 and the final mission. All 12 chapter and mission trophies plus the Gold for the final mission unlock here. Do the Chapter 1 meditation spot for Positive Mental Attitude before Chapter 1 ends.
  2. Stage 2 — Stranger strands. Finish the 8 stranger mission strands (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone and three more) and Bret’s unique-animal hunt. Silver: Strangers & Freaks.
  3. Stage 3 — Activities. Shooting range golds (42), drone races (14 wins), 20 race wins, fishing vs Cal, arcade, bowling, stunts (250), robberies, vaults and the partner trophies.
  4. Stage 4 — Collectibles & exploration. Cards (3 sets), WAiNK scans (100), vehicles (100 discovered, 10 registered), garages (8), all 52 areas of Leonida, the 17 remaining meditation spots, the strange event.
  5. Stage 5 — Cleanup & 100%. 50 challenges, $250,000 laundered, $100,000 spent, then A Long Road (100% completion, Gold) and the Platinum, Vice Got a Price!
