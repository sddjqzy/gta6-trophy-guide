Platinum: Vice Got a Price! — about 200 hours, no online trophies, no difficulty trophies, no mission medals.

The platinum roadmap — five stages

  1. Stage 1 — Chapter 1: protect the missable

    Do Lucia’s balcony meditation before Chapter 1 ends — it feeds Positive Mental Attitude and never comes back. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter; there is no mission replay.

  2. Stage 2 — Play the story

    All 12 story trophies pop as you play, including the gold for the final mission. Character switching opens after Breakfast Run in Chapter 2, which unlocks most of the free-roam list.

  3. Stage 3 — Robberies and partner trophies

    Jewelry stores, bank vaults, armored trucks and shop runs — several need Jason and Lucia together, so do them as a pair and let Hot Together and Retail Therapy fall out of the same jobs.

  4. Stage 4 — Activities and collectibles

    Stunts, races, shooting ranges, drone races, cards, WAiNK scans, vehicles and meditation spots. Fold the counting trophies into your exploring instead of saving them all for the end.

  5. Stage 5 — Cleanup and 100%

    After the credits, work the 100% checklist for A Long Road. Everything outside the story stays available, and the platinum pops the moment the last trophy does.

Missable: Positive Mental Attitude — the Chapter 1 balcony meditation. Never use cheats on your trophy save: they switch off trophies and saving.
